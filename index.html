TDP Practice OA

Practice Online Assessment

A self-contained simulation of the two-part OA format that candidates commonly report for Capital One's Technology Development Program: a timed coding round (bank/business-flavored problems, no whiteboard graph theory or DP) followed by a HireVue-style timed behavioral round.

Round 1 · Coding (70 minutes)

  • 4 questions: Easy, Easy, Medium, Hard — 100 points total.
  • Bank/business-flavored string, simulation, and state-machine problems.
  • Code runs against visible and hidden test cases in a sandboxed Web Worker.
  • No dynamic programming or advanced graph theory — read carefully, model the state.

Round 2 · Behavioral (HireVue-style)

  • 30 seconds of prep time per question, then a timed typed response (stand-in for a recorded answer).
  • 6 STAR-format behavioral questions + 4 motivation / mini-case questions.
  • Use the STAR framework: Situation, Task, Action, Result — and speak in "I", not "we".
  • Self-graded at the end against a STAR checklist.

How this was built

Format and difficulty pattern based on candidate reports aggregated from Reddit / Blind (teamblind.com) threads and interview-prep write-ups describing Capital One's CodeSignal OA and HireVue behavioral round. See README.md in this repo for sources and caveats — treat this as directional practice, not a guarantee of the real assessment's exact content.

Results

Runs entirely in your browser — no code or answers are sent anywhere. Open README.md for details and sources.
